## Full description (4000 chars)
Aziz Academy is a bilingual (English / Arabic) learning playground built for
kids ages 6 to 12. It mixes geography, world capitals, country flags, basic
science, mental math, and brain-training puzzles into short, friendly rounds
that fit between school and dinner.

Designed kid-safe by default:
- 100% offline-first. No accounts. No login. No personal data leaves the device.
- No third-party ads, no in-app purchases, no popups.
- Family profiles let siblings share one device with their own name, age, and
  avatar — while still cheering each other on with shared trophies.
- Parent dashboard hidden behind a simple math gate, with weekly digest, a
  printable progress report, and full settings.

What's inside (130+ activities):
- World Maps Explorer: tap continents, learn famous landmarks
- Capitals Quiz: 150+ countries, multiple difficulties, bilingual
- Flag Quiz: visually beautiful flag identification
- Sciences: 200+ science questions across biology, chemistry, physics,
  astronomy, earth sciences (now with deeper level 2 + 3 packs)
- Math Quiz: addition through multiplication, smart-difficulty
- Early-elementary math: Number Bonds (Make 10 / 20), Place Value
  (tens-rods + ones-cubes), Skip Counting (by 2s / 5s / 10s)
- Shapes Basics: 12 fundamental shapes with sides + real-world examples
- Arabic & English alphabets with example words and emojis
- Brain Boost (formerly IQ): patterns, mental math, analogies, logic, plus
  spatial and memory — 720+ puzzles in total
- Boss Rush: chain three modules in one heroic 12-question run
- Pass and Play: local 2-player mode for siblings or friends
- Weekly Tournament: family-internal leaderboard that resets each week
- Daily Challenge with streak rewards
- Shop with avatars, frames, and streak-freeze insurance — coins are earned
  through play, never bought
- Achievement Trophy Room with shareable certificate
- Islamic suite: 15 short surahs with real-reciter audio, Hadith,
  99 Names of Allah, Prophet Stories, Du'a, Athkar, Tasbih, Tajweed Basics
- Madrasati: school-curriculum study mode and homework helper

Languages: English, Arabic (full RTL).
